# BONYAN Workstream 03 - Training Intelligence

## Purpose
Deliver personalized workout plans, workout execution/logging, exercise demonstrations, deterministic progression, substitutions, and a conversational coach.

## Product Principle
The workout system must remain useful without the LLM. Workout decisions come from a deterministic engine. The coach is a natural-language interface to typed tools and services.

## User Stories
1. Generate a workout plan from goal, experience, schedule, equipment, preferences, and confirmed profile data.
2. Open today's workout.
3. Watch the correct exercise demonstration.
4. Log sets, reps, and weight quickly in the gym.
5. Complete a workout.
6. Receive deterministic progression guidance.
7. Substitute an exercise when equipment is unavailable.
8. Ask training-related questions to the coach.
9. Let the coach use current plan/history without inventing facts.

## Non-Goals
- Medical diagnosis or rehab prescription.
- Nutrition planning.
- Live voice coach.
- Multi-agent coach.
- Embeddings/vector DB.
- Recommendation ML.
- Autonomous background agents.
